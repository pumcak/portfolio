Introduction

My name is Timéo Chabal and I am a second-year student on the BUT in Computer Science at IUT Clermont Auvergne, on the Aubière campus. The BUT is a three-year French undergraduate degree that pairs coursework with team projects. I came to development after a general Baccalauréat in Mathematics and Physics-Chemistry, passed with merit, out of a taste for science subjects and problem solving.

My specialisation is mobile application development, within the track called “Application development: design, build, validate”. In practice, I am learning to build an application end to end: understand a need, model the data, write the interface and the business logic, then test it and ship it.

What I can build

Through my coursework projects and my own side projects, I have learned to build complete applications rather than exercises:

Mobile apps: cross-platform interfaces in C# / .NET MAUI and XAML, my second-year specialisation
Desktop apps: Onitama, a board game built in a pair, with a full graphical interface and complete game logic
Command-line tools: a stock management tool in C, with the data saved to files
Websites: responsive HTML / CSS, including this portfolio, written and deployed by hand
Databases: relational modelling, writing SQL queries, joins and aggregates
Back-end & automation: a Discord bot in Node.js consuming a REST API, Bash scripts on Linux

Four things I bring

Rigour
Checking and testing before shipping, rather than hoping nobody looks.
Autonomy

Teamwork
